# HotpotQA Dataset Attribution

## Aggregate-only release boundary

The HotpotQA-related materials in this repository release sanitized aggregate results and metadata only. They do not redistribute HotpotQA original questions, answers, titles, paragraphs, sample identifiers, candidate records, selected-evidence records, predictions, responses, or per-example records. The strict supporting-fact materials are not an end-to-end rerun package and do not distribute the benchmark itself.

The underlying benchmark remains available through its official distribution channels and remains subject to its original terms and attribution requirements.

## Required source attribution

When reporting or reusing the scoped aggregate material, retain an appropriate scholarly reference to:

> Yang, Z., Qi, P., Zhang, S., Bengio, Y., Cohen, W. W., Salakhutdinov, R., and Manning, C. D. HotpotQA: A Dataset for Diverse, Explainable Multi-hop Question Answering. In *Proceedings of EMNLP* (2018).

Official sources:

- [HotpotQA project page](https://hotpotqa.github.io/)
- [HotpotQA official repository](https://github.com/hotpotqa/hotpot)
- [HotpotQA paper, ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/D18-1259/)
- [Creative Commons Attribution–ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)

The official HotpotQA project page states that the dataset is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. The repository’s CC BY-SA scope for sanitized aggregate outputs is limited to the authors’ own rights in the listed contributions. It does not make a legal conclusion that all forms of aggregate-result redistribution are permitted, and it does not waive or replace the benchmark’s original terms. Users and authors should review the current official terms and any applicable institutional or other restrictions.
