# Licence Scope and Remaining Release Checks

The legacy filename is retained for traceability. It no longer means that all licences are undecided. The authors have confirmed the following path-scoped treatment for author-owned contributions in 2026:

- author-original Python scripts: MIT, where listed in `HOT_POTQA_LICENSE_SCOPE.md` or `SHARED_ROOT_MATERIALS_SCOPE.md`;
- author-original documents and figures: CC BY 4.0, where listed in the applicable scope file;
- scoped HotpotQA sanitized aggregate results and configuration metadata: CC BY-SA 4.0;
- five directly scoped MuSiQue aggregate/configuration/figure assets: CC BY 4.0.

There is intentionally no single repository-wide `LICENSE`, because these licences apply by path and do not grant third-party rights. `COPYRIGHT_NOTICE.md`, `HOT_POTQA_LICENSE_SCOPE.md`, `MUSIQUE_LICENSE_SCOPE.md`, and `SHARED_ROOT_MATERIALS_SCOPE.md` are controlling scope records.

Before a public release, the authors must still resolve the files marked `PUBLIC-EXCLUDED / AUTHOR-CONFIRMATION-REQUIRED` in `SHARED_ROOT_MATERIALS_SCOPE.md`, verify any institutional, funder, collaborator, or publisher conditions, and complete any final repository, release-tag, DOI, or Data/Code Availability metadata.
