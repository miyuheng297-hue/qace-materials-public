# MuSiQue Dataset Attribution

## Aggregate-only boundary

The MuSiQue-scoped assets in this repository provide sanitized aggregate table/figure values, aggregate statistical summaries, configuration metadata, and an author-produced Figure 3 raster asset. They do not redistribute MuSiQue original questions, paragraphs, titles, answers, sample identifiers, candidate pools, selected-paragraph records, predictions, responses, logs, or per-example records.

The scoped material is not an end-to-end MuSiQue rerun package and must not be described as a redistribution of the benchmark or its model inputs.

## Official source attribution

When reporting or reusing the scoped material, retain an appropriate scholarly reference to:

> Trivedi, H., Balasubramanian, N., Khot, T., and Sabharwal, A. MuSiQue: Multihop Questions via Single-hop Question Composition. *Transactions of the Association for Computational Linguistics* 10, 539–554 (2022). https://doi.org/10.1162/tacl_a_00475.
