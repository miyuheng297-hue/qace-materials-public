# Claim boundary

## Retain

- SP precision, recall, F1, and average selected-evidence count over 7,405 items.
- Comparisons under one audited shared frozen candidate pool.
- The 10 predefined contrasts, 20,000 paired-bootstrap replicates, and the three declared Holm families.

## Downgrade

- Describe findings as SP-only evidence-selection effects under a frozen pool.
- Describe component and sensitivity results as family-adjusted statistical associations, not universal model-quality or causal claims.

## Remove

- Any answer-generation, Answer EM/F1, Joint EM/F1, Official Joint, or end-to-end QA claim.
- Any claim that this package freshly invoked a retriever, BGE, selector, answer model, or external service.
- Any significance statement outside the 10 predefined contrasts and declared Holm families.
